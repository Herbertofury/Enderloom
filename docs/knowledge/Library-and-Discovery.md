# Discovery, favorites and content

[Home](Home.md) / [Checklist](Checklist.md) / [Architecture](Architecture.md) / [Ecosystem](Ecosystem.md) / [Source map](Source-Map.md)

> Research a project, act on it, and return to its evidence without losing context.

### Broad source-aware discovery

[**LIB-01**](Checklist.md#lib-01) - Preserve the full provider universe and real project identities, source links, creator attribution, all logical results and source health; native APIs/session-aware fallbacks remain truthful.

### Correct premium media and trailers

[**LIB-02**](Checklist.md#lib-02) - Keep project icons/avatars/gallery/post/video roles distinct, exact-project ownership, full-resolution media/lightbox and source-grounded autoplay trailer preferences; no synthetic replacements or unrelated promotions.

### Real embedded browser

[**LIB-03**](Checklist.md#lib-03) - Preserve Chromium tabs, persistent authorized sessions, real login, split/full/source modes, filtered networking and safe translation/original toggles without fake static pages.

### Catalog-to-instance bridge

[**LIB-04**](Checklist.md#lib-04) - Install from research, choose exact compatible versions and multiple instances, review dependencies and reopen focused research from installed content.

### Dedicated Favorites workspace

[**LIB-05**](Checklist.md#lib-05) - Favorites independent of installation, tags/groups/notes, grid/list/table, full-dataset bulk actions, latest/tested/installed states and exact-context actions persist; unfavorite never uninstalls.

### Content lifecycle and provenance

[**LIB-06**](Checklist.md#lib-06) - Manage mods/resource packs/shaders/data packs, manual and provider-backed content, enable/disable/remove/update with snapshots, dependents, exact file hashes and preserved local configs.

### Exact versions, freeze and upgrades

[**LIB-07**](Checklist.md#lib-07) - Search full compatible/incompatible version choices, changelogs, pin/freeze/unfreeze and rollback; frozen content stays fixed unless explicitly changed.

### Provider downloads and account recovery

[**LIB-08**](Checklist.md#lib-08) - Respect author-disabled third-party downloads, safe browser handoff and verified adoption, reconnect legitimate sessions and resume exact jobs without guessed identities or bypass.

### Pack and research interchange

[**LIB-09**](Checklist.md#lib-09) - Support accepted MRPack/CurseForge ZIP/packwiz and CSV/JSON/XLSX/HTML/PDF research interchange with provenance, original fields and clickable links; no cloned proprietary share-code service.
