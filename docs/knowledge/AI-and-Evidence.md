# AI operator and evidence brain

> One job and one evidence model, regardless of who drives the interface.

### Authorized provider lanes

[**AI-01**](Checklist.md#ai-01) - Integrate supported configured OpenAI API/Agents, embedded Codex, authenticated ChatGPT browser and MCP lanes without confusing subscription entitlement with API billing or requiring a new paid service for ordinary actions.

### Natural-language job compiler

[**AI-02**](Checklist.md#ai-02) - Resolve project/intent/target, compile scoped typed domain actions, expose progress and execute full app capabilities through the same validation/storage/runtime contract.

### Quarantined changes and repair loop

[**AI-03**](Checklist.md#ai-03) - Inspect returned source/binaries, verify identities/hashes, stage changes, build/test, feed exact failures back to the authorized thread and iterate; model confidence cannot approve itself.

### Approval, privacy and quality controls

[**AI-04**](Checklist.md#ai-04) - Keep secrets out of bundles/logs, explain outbound context and necessary approvals, respect user quality choices and legitimate authentication/challenges, and resume after required user action.

### Parallel specialists and context reuse

[**AI-05**](Checklist.md#ai-05) - Share stable project context, dependency ownership and exact jobs across AI/local work, reuse valid prompts/artifacts and avoid racing source writes or repeated rediscovery.

### Evidence Brain promotion and rollback

[**AI-06**](Checklist.md#ai-06) - Learn only source-bound verified fixes/contracts, challenge/generalize/shadow-test with applicability and invalidation, prevent poisoned guesses, audit and roll back learned rules.

### AI evaluation and observability

[**AI-07**](Checklist.md#ai-07) - Record model/provider/tool versions, costs/quotas when actually available, iterations/errors/outcomes and quality fixtures; no hidden truncation, fabricated unlimited access or unverified capability claims.
